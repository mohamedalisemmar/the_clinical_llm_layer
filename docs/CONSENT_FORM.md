## What the study is about
We are studying how clinicians read and assess answers produced by an artificial-intelligence (AI) system
for clinical cases, and whether a different way of displaying those answers helps clinicians judge them.

## What you will do
You will review about 30 short clinical cases on a computer, each accompanied by an AI-generated answer.
For each case you will decide whether you would accept the AI's recommendation and answer a few short
questions. You will also complete short questionnaires about usability and workload. The session takes
about 45 to 60 minutes.

## Important information
* All cases are **fictional (synthetic)**. No real patient data are used.
* Some AI answers are deliberately incorrect. This is part of the study design.
* This is not a test of your clinical knowledge. There is no penalty for any answer.

## Your rights
* Participation is voluntary. You may stop at any time without giving a reason.
* Your answers are stored under a code, never with your name.
* Only anonymised, aggregated results will appear in the internship report or any publication.
* You may ask for your data to be deleted until [date].
